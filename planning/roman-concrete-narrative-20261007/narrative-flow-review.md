# Narrative flow review — v8 is readable, but reads as a catalogue

## Evidence and boundary
Reviewed actual figma_poster_v8_1x.png and current reel-analysis.md. This is a cognitive-flow design diagnosis, not measured attention, reader comprehension, or retention evidence. Earlier v8 PASS established no observed visual clipping/collision; it did not establish success at Reel rhetoric or user acceptance. No Figma writes, model changes, map changes or central Colosseum changes.

## Why it reads as a catalogue
- The largest first message is the topic name 로마 콘크리트. The question is subordinate. Top-left recipe and top-right research/context are simultaneously presented before the CORE opening; research appears before the mechanism.
- CORE chapters all have the same number/title/body/underline pattern. The heading/body pairs define facts: different binder, calcium storage, crack filling, experiment. They do not make the reader carry an unresolved question into the next object.
- C1 names different binders but does not visually expose a comparable pair of recipes. The top-left ingredients are a Roman-only list, separated from the modern frame and C1 contrast.
- C2 has correct action wording, but mixing order remains two small prose lines below a heading; the image is a bowl, not an explicit before/after action chain.
- C3 gives away calcium storage in its heading. It misses the Reel move from apparently poorly mixed white grains to a useful calcium source.
- C4 has the best sequential image, but its three cubes have no state-specific text. The general heading and general lower caption force the reader to infer which visual element means water, calcium transfer and new crystal filling.
- C5 is physically above C3/C4 and research is already introduced in the top-right panel. The final evidence therefore does not feel like a payoff. The timeline records events; no sentence returns to the opening question.

## Four bounded overlay changes

### 1. Make the opening a contrast, and make C1 a comparable recipe pair
Keep map/model/arena anchors. Give the question/hook the first visual voice, and demote the topic/context labels. The current giant title can become a short hook such as 철근도 없는데? with 로마 콘크리트 retained as smaller topic identification, subject to root keeping any exact title requirement. Keep the accepted full question beneath. At C1, use a short bridge such as 같은 콘크리트, / 배합부터 달랐다, then two matched rows, not a Roman-only ingredient list:
- 현대의 대표 배합: 시멘트 + 골재 + 물
- 연구된 로마 배합: 생석회 + 화산재 + 골재 + 물
Use binder words in bold and common 골재/물 in lighter text. Keep the iron-tension line explicitly separate from the recipe pair; reinforcement and binder are different comparisons.
Coordinate candidates: current C1 body area x528/y446, extend width only to about 170 pt, two 10–10.5 pt rows with 14 pt line height; iron note remains x528/y482 if rows finish before y474. A short opening bridge also fits the narrow blank gap around [400,240,820,258], but choose it instead of adding competing top text. Top-right research intro should be demoted or hidden as overlay text, with the substantive research payoff reserved for C5.

### 2. Give C2 an action question and an explicit order
Replace category-style repetition with a transition: 물은 언제 넣었을까? / 마른 재료를 섞은 다음. Then show a compact action chain in the current two body lines: 생석회·화산재를 마른 채 혼합 → 물 첨가 → 발열·빠른 굳음. If the line is too wide, use the first line for the initial ingredients and the second for 물 첨가 → 열 발생 → 굳음. This is observed process order, not a claim that mixing order alone explains all Roman survival.
Coordinate candidates: current C2 heading/body box [69,756,217,844]; optional small transition line in the gap [70,736,225,750] if it remains clear after font measurement. No arrows across the arena: connect the actions locally beside the bowl.

Recommended. That missing rhetorical transfer is the specific gap to repair; no-clipping QA does not close it.
