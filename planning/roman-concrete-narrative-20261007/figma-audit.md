## Delivery bottlenecks from current visual/content evidence
1. **The largest message names the subject; the explanatory promise is secondary.** “로마 콘크리트” has size 80, “어떻게 2천 년을 버텼을까?” size 24. It attracts attention to a large object/topic before posing a mystery. A benchmark hook can replace or subordinate the subject label while retaining it as an eyebrow/title.
2. **The map supports roaming more strongly than serial explanation.** Mountain clusters, fields, temples, aqueduct and volcano all invite equal exploration. Number ① is not at an obvious entry edge; reaching ② requires a large diagonal move; ③④ sit below the central object while ⑤ returns to the right. The paved road helps, but viewers still select the next module themselves. Spatial separation is doing the job that cuts/timed captions would do in video.
3. **The decisive process is visually small.** Three gray crack cubes at bottom are the transformation that explains the surprising material behavior. They compete with a huge Colosseum and a volcano. Gray stages have weak before/after differentiation, while narration/captions would tell a video viewer what changed. Enlarging just the process, its crack/water/nodule evidence and its proof comparison provides more explanatory benefit than adding scenery.
4. **The payoff repeats the starting fact.** ⑤ says it still stands; it does not state what caused the endurance. Older `585:78` had “한 가지 비결이 아니다” and “재료·만들기·짓기·고치기가 함께 맞물려 2천 년을 버텼다.” Those have disappeared from v6/v7 text. Reintroducing a concise answer completes the question → explanation → answer arc.
5. **Repair is conflated with self-healing in the abridgment.** ④ headline promises both “메우고” and “쓰면서 고침”, but body only explains lime-driven filling. Human repair appears as “19세기 벽돌 버팀벽” in the small bottom timeline. The Colosseum's ruined geometry is excellent evidence for a “remaining does not mean undamaged” point, but the relation needs a direct caption.
6. **Evidence boundaries were removed with text shortening.** `585:1458` and `585:1469` explicitly say self-healing comes from other sites/recreated experiments and was not directly confirmed in Colosseum; v7 has neither. Proximity of self-healing chapter to the giant Colosseum can imply direct proof there. Restore one nearby concise scope label and distinguish “건물 사례” from “재료/실험 근거.”
7. **Internal analysis labels consume visual attention.** “중심 · 질문”, “전제”, and recurrent “원리” describe the planner's information roles. Plain audience prompts can make the map less procedural: e.g. “무엇으로 만들었나”, “흰 알갱이가 남았다”, “금 속에서 무슨 일이 일어났나”, “실험에서 확인한 것은”.
8. **Proof is buried.** `634:923` already contains the strongest tiny experiment comparison: “금으로 흐르던 물이 2주 안에 멈춤, 알갱이 없으면 계속 샘.” It sits near the bottom in 9.5-size text. A matched pair with the same crack/water setup and a large “2주” result could become the static equivalent of a video reveal. This is an existing caption, not independently verified in this lane.
9. **Marine and terrestrial mechanisms need a visible bridge.** Sea-water crystal growth currently appears at upper-left DETAIL and harbour SUPPORT, while main hero is an inland Colosseum. Current headline ① is broad. A short “항만 시료에서” qualifier and distinct mechanism path prevent readers treating all marine findings as directly describing that building.
