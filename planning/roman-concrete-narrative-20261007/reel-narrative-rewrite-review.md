## What v8 loses from the Reel

v8 retained the scientific topic order but removed the rhetorical work that makes one step create the next. Its five headings are category summaries: binder difference / hot mixing / calcium store / crack filling / experimental result. The supporting text repeats those categories. A reader can understand any block without wondering what comes next.

## Proposed five CORE passages

These are original editorial passages based on the inspected sources, not a reproduction of the Reel transcript. Line breaks are proposed text lines, not a guarantee of fit inside the existing node widths. Keep body type readable and let root adjust the text frames around fixed imagery.

### CORE1 — familiar damage → surprise → the difference to investigate

Headline:

> 철근이 있어도  
> 금은 생기는데

Body, three lines:

> 그런데 로마는 철근 없이도  
> 약2천 년 전 유구가 남아 있다.  
> 둘을 굳히는 재료는 무엇이 다를까?

Tactical caption at the modern building / material comparison:

> 대표 결합재 — 현대: 시멘트 / 로마: 석회·화산재

The central question already introduces Roman survival. C1 deliberately picks that question up and turns it into a material investigation. The caption compares representative binder systems; it does not imply modern recipes never include pozzolans. The existing modern steel frame can stay, but it must not be labelled as a failure specimen.

### CORE2 — recipe → action → the grain left behind
